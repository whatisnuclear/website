DOE Reactor Pilot Program

Aug 2025: DOE picked 11 advanced reactor projects. Goal: 3 test reactors critical by July 4, 2026, under DOE (not NRC) authorization.

Reactor Where Critical
Antares Mark-0 INL, Idaho Jun 2026
Valar Atomics Ward 250 Utah San Rafael Energy Lab Jun 2026
Deployable Energy Unity INL, Idaho Jun 30, 2026
Aalo Atomics Aalo-X INL, Idaho Jul 4, 2026
Oklo Groves Isotope Test Reactor Lockhart, Texas Aug 2026

Five new reactors critical in one summer: goal beaten! (Mostly zero-power tests; full-power operation comes next.) (DOE)

Plus: Aug 2026, the Army's Janus program picked Antares, BWXT, General Atomics, Radiant and Westinghouse to build microreactors at five Army bases (Army)

Before this, the last new US test reactor designs went critical decades ago. Valar went beyond zero power. Deployable Energy came in through DOE's follow-on Nuclear Energy Launch Pad program.
